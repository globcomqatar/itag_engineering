# Training: Engineering Requestors

**Role:** Engineering Requestor (and the other originating-department roles who may submit on their own behalf: Production Planner, Production Supervisor, Quality Engineer, Sales User, Procurement User, Stores User, Workshop Technician — see `ecr_service.SUBMIT_ROLES`).

## What you do in this system
You raise requests for new items and for changes to existing engineering records. You do NOT approve, release, or execute any of these — your role ends at submission.

## What you'll actually use
1. **Engineering Item Request** — when a new part/valve configuration is needed. Fill in the description and specification; submit for review. You cannot reserve the Item Code or create the Item yourself — that's Engineering Manager's step.
2. **Engineering Change Request** — when something existing needs to change. You must fill in a real Problem Statement, Requested Change, and at least one affected object (Item/Drawing/BOM/Routing) — the system rejects submission without these.
3. If Engineering Manager sends your ECR back for "More Information Required", you'll see their comment on the document's timeline — respond by editing and resubmitting ("Resubmit for Review").

## What you will NOT be able to do (by design)
- Approve your own EIR or ECR.
- Reserve an Item Code or create the resulting Item.
- Accept an ECR into an ECO, or approve any ECO step.

## Where to check status
**Engineering Item Request Register**, **ECR Aging and Status** — both show your submissions and their current state.
